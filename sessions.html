1-1 Sessions

Personal Healing Sessions with Sal

Personalised healing, tailored to you.

No two people carry the same story in their body, so no two sessions with me look quite the same. Each session is a holistic look at your emotional, physical, and spiritual health together, rather than treating any one in isolation.

How It Works

Using kinesiology and my channelling connection, I am able to pinpoint where an issue is really coming from — whether that's diet, emotional trauma, energetic imbalance, or something else entirely. From there, I draw on whichever combination of tools fits what you need, which may include:

  • Dietary changes
  • Supplements
  • Bach flower, homoeopathic remedies, tissue salts
  • Emotional clearing
  • Trauma exploration
  • Chakric release
  • Cranial realignment
  • Energy meridian work
  • Acupressure (no needles)
  • Physical exercises
  • Past life emotional release
  • Relationship shadow work

...and a number of other holistic approaches, depending on what comes up.

Nothing is prescribed in advance — I listen to what your body, your soul, and your history are telling me, and the session unfolds from there.

What to Expect

Most people find that real, lasting shifts take a little time to unfold, so I generally recommend starting with three sessions. This gives space to properly identify what's going on and to work through it in layers, rather than just addressing what's on the surface.
