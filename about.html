About

Sal March

35 years walking the path of the healer.

I've spent 35 years walking the path of the healer. Over that time I've trained and practised across a vast range of modalities:

  • Spiritual Healing
  • Gestalt Psychotherapy
  • Acupuncture (both Traditional Chinese Medicine and Five Element traditions)
  • Cranio-Sacral Therapy
  • Daoist internal practices
  • Chinese internal martial arts
  • Applied Kinesiology
  • Massage
  • Brandon's Journey therapy

This grounding gives my work a rare depth: I move fluently between the physical, emotional, and energetic layers of a person, rather than treating them as separate.

Ego & Soul

At the heart of everything I teach is the relationship between Ego and Soul. I work with people to release the trauma, suppressed emotion, and old survival patterns held in the body - the "ego structures" we build to protect ourselves. When required, we also revisit past lives to release karmic bonds that have cast a long shadow into your present life, so that you truly may shine once more.

Central threads in my work are: shadow work, emotional release through the chakras and the dismantling of protective armour and limiting beliefs.This all builds up in the body causing physical pain, discomfort and disease. It's this ongoing exploration that gave the Ego & Soul Integration work its name: aligning the ego with the soul, rather than them being in conflict with one another.

A Channel

Alongside this, I'm also a channel. For several years I've been in regular communication with a higher-dimensional soul I call "Charley" and more recently with a highly evolved consciousness I refer to as "The Collective." Through this connection, I share teachings on the nature of the soul, humanity's place within a much larger cosmic story, and what I understand to be a pivotal window for spiritual awakening in the years ahead.

For me, the healing work and the channelling aren't separate paths but two expressions of the same inquiry - into who we really are beneath the ego, and what we're here to remember.

I bring this same blend of grounded practitioner experience and expanded perspective into everything I teach.
