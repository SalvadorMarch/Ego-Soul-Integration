Course 2

The Self Healing Path

A 24-week online exploration into your subconscious.

For those who have completed Course 1: The Self Discovery Path, this more advanced 24-week (six month) programme is available. This is a deeper, group therapy-based exploration of the patterns, wounds, and unconscious motivations underneath it all.

∞Anywhere in the world

What You'll Receive

  • Group call: twice per month for 2 hours per session
  • A blend of online group therapy and focused individual assignments
  • A community space: to share your experiences and thoughts with your peers
  • Optional Addition: 1-1 sessions with Sal

In an age of instant gratification and endless screen scrolling, a six-month commitment of your time, focus, and energy can feel daunting. This level of dedication is not for everyone. To qualify, you must have already completed Course 1: The Self Discovery Path, our 12-week introductory programme.

We will have a private community space where you can connect and support each other as you progress.

Between the group therapy sessions, there will be tasks and exercises to carry out. These aim to highlight aspects of your perceptions, beliefs, and inner-wiring, as well as connect you with your higher self.

Why Group Therapy?

Every two weeks, we will meet online to share our thoughts, concerns, and questions. Together, we will create an uncompromisingly safe space to dive deep into your perceptions, inner scripts, emotions, and whatever else is holding you back.

You might be thinking, this sounds like psychotherapy, why not just do one-to-one sessions?

While individual therapy may indeed become a necessity for some of you at some point, do not dismiss the profound power of collective witnessing. Sharing in, and partaking of others' pain serves as a powerful mirror for our own inner disharmonies. Group therapy is a catalyst; it expedites self-healing by showing us we are not alone in the dark.

Sacred Trust in the Group

Therapy sessions are strictly confidential and are not to be discussed with anyone outside of the active members of this group. Every participant's privacy, history, and vulnerability must be treated with the utmost respect. Before we begin we will all sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) to enforce this boundary.

To help you integrate this work, sessions are typically recorded so you can re-experience and reflect on them. However, we recognise that some wounds are exceptionally deep, and some past truths are highly sensitive to expose.

Your psychological safety is our absolute priority. If a participant needs to share an unpalatable truth or a deeply buried secret and requests that no record of it exist, we will pause recording. We respect the need to leave some things entirely in the moment, shared only with the living witnesses of the group, leaving no digital footprint behind.
